Our Story

A track record built over two decades.

CyberLearning was founded in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh in 2006 with a clear purpose: to make world-class Microsoft certification training accessible across India and beyond. What began as a focused training operation has grown into a programme delivery partner that governments, universities, and enterprises trust for high-stakes capability-building.

We are a Microsoft Global Training Partner — a standing earned through consistent pass rates, programme rigour, and the kind of delivery experience that only comes from doing this — at every scale — for twenty years.

Our headquarters remain in Greater Noida. Our work spans five continents.

Landmark Programme · Mauritius

Training a third of a nation.

In our most significant programme to date, CyberLearning partnered with the Government of Mauritius to deliver a national digital literacy initiative that reached approximately 33% of the entire population — a programme completed over a decade ago.

This was not a pilot or a proof of concept. It was a full-scale sovereign engagement: programme design, curriculum development, trainer deployment, testing centre management, and outcome reporting — at national scale.

Very few training organisations globally can point to work of this scope. We don't lead with it because we think it makes us sound impressive. We share it because it's true, and because it shapes how we think about every programme since.
